5. AI and automated decision-making

5.1 Use of artificial intelligence

We use AI to generate content proposals based on your brand guidelines and instructions.

5.2 Human oversight

You retain full control over all content at all times:

  • All AI-generated content must be approved by you via our 5-gate system
  • Human intervention is always required before content is published
  • You can reject, edit, or approve every proposal

5.3 No fully automated decision-making

We do not make fully automated decisions with legal or significant consequences for you.

5.4 AI training

We do not use your data to train third-party AI models. Your data is strictly separated from training datasets.

9. Security of your data

9.1 Technical measures

  • Encryption in transit: all data transmitted via HTTPS/TLS
  • Encryption at rest: database encryption for stored data
  • Access control: role-based access control (RBAC)
  • Authentication: two-factor authentication (2FA) mandatory for employees
  • Logging: comprehensive audit logs of all data access
  • Backups: regular, encrypted backups
  • Penetration tests: annual security assessments

9.2 Organisational measures

  • Confidentiality agreements for all employees
  • Regular security awareness training
  • Incident response plan
  • Data Protection Impact Assessment (DPIA)

10. Your rights

Under the GDPR you have the following rights:

10.1 Right of access

You have the right to know what personal data we process about you.

10.2 Right to rectification

You can ask us to correct or supplement inaccurate data.

10.3 Right to erasure ("right to be forgotten")

You can ask us to delete your data, unless we have a legal obligation to retain it.

10.4 Right to restriction

You can ask us to temporarily freeze the processing of your data.

10.5 Right to data portability

You have the right to receive your data in a structured, machine-readable format.

10.6 Right to object

You can object to processing based on legitimate interest.

10.7 Right to human intervention regarding AI

You can request human review of AI-generated content or decisions.

10.8 Exercising your rights
